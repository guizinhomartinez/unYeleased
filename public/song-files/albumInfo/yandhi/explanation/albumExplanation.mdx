Yandhi is an infamous scrapped album by Ye that is arguably his most famous unreleased project so far.
Supposed to release September 29th 2018, the project would be continuously pushed back, and would be evolved into what we know as JESUS IS KING.
\
\
The production of this album began in the Good Ass Job (the Chance The Rapper version, not the 2009 one) recording sessions,
which is when the first version of Hurricane was produced.
\
After Ye heard Hurricane, he began the production of the whole concept of the album.
After this, he would work relentlessly on the album during September of 2018.
\
In September 27th 2018, Ye would go on Twitter and would announce that the album Yandhi was releasing only 2 days from now (September 29th).
This event also coincided with him appearing at the SNL. Unfortunately,
the album would not release, and only Kim would acknowledge this,
saying that the album was not supposed to release on November 23rd.
\
From September to November, Ye would go to Uganda, to work on the project, and after returning home he would announce that the album was delayed indefinitely.
\
In the album's songs, Ye included a wide variety of artists to participate in songs, such as 070 Shake, Ant Clemons, Nicki Minaj, Ty Dolla $ign etc.
\
\
After the album was delayed indefinitely,Ye would collaborate with Timbaland to create a "more healing music" for the album.
This coincided with the sudden change of Ye's attitude towards life, taking a more conservative and christian approach.
This would be the era where Ye would create the Sunday Service Choir, 2019.
\
\
By mid-2019, the album had evolved to what we know as JESUS IS KING.
In this album, only three songs from Yandhi would appear,
and these songs received massive reworks to fit the christian theme.
\
\
Although Yandhi evolved into JESUS IS KING, songs from this era would appear in other projects.
Hurricane, for example, would reappear in Donda; Sky City also reappeared in VULTURES 2, although unfinished and with AI used as Ye's vocals.
