Bad Bitch Playbook is an early version of VULTURES 1 that was recorded in Japan and Italy by Ye and Ty Dolla $ign.
\
\
Following the infamous controversies Ye had in 2022, he stayed away from the public away, and quietly produced YEBU, in Japan.
While still producing the album, in 2023, Ty Dolla $ign travelled to Japan to work with Ye, and for him to produce his, now called, album TYCOON.
\
To persuade Ye into producing his album, Ty showed some songs already made, one of them being BURN,
which Ye reportedly loved so much he decided to make a completely new collaborative album featuring him and Ty.
\
\
In late July, Ye and Ty Dolla $ign travelled to Italy to continue working on the album.
\
\
The public would discover the album's existence after TMZ reported Ye was working on a solo album and maybe even a collab album with Ty Dolla $ign.
\
On October 1st, a private listening party was held, where Ye and Ty previewed some new songs with fully recorded vocals.
\
\
Finally, on October 5th, Ty confirmed the new album by posting a picture on Instagram with the caption "¥$".
The album was supposed to be released on October 13th, but because Ty and Ye had no distributing partner, the album was postponed.
\
Not much longer after confirming the album existence, the whole YZY team relocated to Saudi Arabia to continue working on the album,
and it is believed that at this point the album had been reworked to be what we now know as VULTURES.
\
\
