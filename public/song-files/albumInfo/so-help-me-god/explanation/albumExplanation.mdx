_So Help Me God_ is an unreleased project that was worked on during October 2014 and May 2015 before being scrapped and reworked into _SWISH_ and later on _The Life Of Pablo_.
Ye released three singles before scrapping this album: "All Day", "Only One", and "FourFiveSeconds"
\
\
After the release of _Yeezus_, Ye started producing _Yeezus 2_, but, by fall of 2014, the project had went through a lot of changes and was now known as _So Help Me God_.
\
In 2014, in September, a private listening party was held, and, on December 31st, "Only One" was released as a single. On January 24, 2015, "FourFiveSeconds" was also released as a single.
\
On February 12, at the Yeezy Season 1 fashion show, an early version of "Wolves" was played as the opening track for his new album. On Feburary 20, Kanye announced that the new album was about 80% done. Just 8 days later, Kanye also announced the official title of his new project: So Help Me God.
\
The album seemed to be gearing up to be released, with another single being released on March 3, called "All Day", but, by May 2015, Ye announced on Twitter that the album would be now called _SWISH_. Only a couple of songs from this era would survive and be released on _The Life Of Pablo_.
