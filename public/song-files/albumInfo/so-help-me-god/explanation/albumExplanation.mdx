*So Help Me God* is an unreleased project that was worked on during October 2014 and May 2015 before being scrapped and reworked into *SWISH* and later on *The Life Of Pablo*.
Ye released three singles before scrapping this album: "All Day", "Only One", and "FourFiveSeconds"
\
\
After the release of *Yeezus*, Ye started producing *Yeezus 2*, but, by fall of 2014, the project had went through a lot of changes and was now known as *So Help Me God*.
