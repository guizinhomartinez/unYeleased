Following Ye's tenth album, Donda, he started producing the sequel to this amazing album. The following album was to be called Donda II, but ended
up not being released on any streaming platforms, and even though there was a official release in the Stem Player, many people consider this album
completely unreleased.
\
\
This album was going to be really distinct from other projects, as it was supposed to be produced entirely by Future, and many songs would be made
during this era. Unfortunately, the album was met with mostly negative reviews, calling it incomplete and just a way for Ye to get money by selling
the Stem Player. Following this, the project would not be heard of again, and is presumed to be completely scrapped.
\
\
Most of the work was done between January and February of 2022, and artists like The Game, A$AP Rocky, DJ Khaled were present during some recording
sessions. Unfortunately, a little after these recording sessions, Ye would go on Instagram and make multiple posts against Pete Davidson, and would
even release two singles against him.
\
On February 17th, Ye would confirm the album would be a Stem Player exclusive, which was at the time 200 dolars. He justified this decision by saying
it was meant to redirect the money more fairly to the artists participating.
\
A little after these declarations, Ye would make an event called Donda Experience Performance, where he would preview almost the whole album, but due
to problems with the audio, he would get so frustrated during the event he would even throw his microphone.
\
\
The album was supposed to be released on February 22th, but Ye would not release it on time and would release 4 songs the day after. After that, some
other songs, like "True Love", would be added to the temporary album, but some would be cut off. The album's exclusivity would really harm it, as many
fans did not like this decision at all, and some would even illegaly redistribute it on platforms like YouTube for free.
\
\
More recently a famous song from Donda 2, "530", was redone and added to VULTURES 2, so some fans have speculated that maybe Ye has greater ideas for
this project. To continue this speculatation, Ye would even post this tweet:
